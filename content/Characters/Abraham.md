# Abraham

**Player:** Michael
**Archetype:** Writer
**Key Attribute:** Empathy
**Key Skill:** Inspiration
**Talents:** Automatic Writing, Journalist, Wordsmith
**Resources:** 2–5

## Description
A published author of occult and occult-adjacent novels. Hungry for new vaesen and occult knowledge — every mystery is potential material. Carries writing utensils and a walking stick (used as a cane).

## Notable Actions & Moments
- Found *The Shining Fiend* in the castle library (key lore text)
- Fainted from fear when the green ghost lights appeared in the library
- Smacked Niklas on the butt with his walking stick to snap him out of a ghost-light trance
- Searched the castle library and found forbidden cult texts
- Told Sigrund Hierta he was a theater critic to deflect suspicion; proposed collaboration on a novel
- Helped identify that Ulf's body had been dead only a few days and was screaming when he died
- Tried to inspire Roland Hertdig to leave the castle (failed)
- Attempted to figure out next steps after finding Oskar's bones; rolled badly and gained the condition: Hopelessness
- Realized the party needed to carry Oskar's bones over the cemetery wall (not through the gate) to consecrated ground
