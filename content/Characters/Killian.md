# Killian

**Player:** Birq
**Archetype:** Occultist
**Key Attribute:** Precision
**Key Skill:** Stealth
**Talents:** Magic Tricks, Medium, Striking Fear
**Resources:** 1–4

## Description
A Thursday's Child with strong occult instincts and a hunger to understand the supernatural. Carries a crystal ball and uses it actively in the field.

## Notable Actions & Moments
- First to interrogate Linnea about the green-glowing vaesen at Castle Gyllencreutz
- Used the Ouija board in the séance parlor, triggering a vision of the basement (body, cabinet, glowing barrel)
- Used the crystal ball to reveal secrets of the castle
- Took a potshot at Manfred (missed)
- Helped Niklas contain Dixon when he was entranced by the ghost light
- Picked the lock on the servant's footlocker (found a monkey skull)
- Lit Dixon's absinthe Molotov cocktail when the rag fizzled

## Relationships
- Member of [[The Society]], based at [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]
- Allied with [[Linnea Elfeklint]], [[Abraham]], [[Niklas Natt]], [[Dixon Hill]]
- Contact: [[Sigrund Hierta]] (journalist, Uppsala)
