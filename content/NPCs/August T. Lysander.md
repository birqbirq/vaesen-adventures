# August T. Lysander

## Overview
Wealthy Stockholm occultist and client for Adventure 3. Owner of Lysander Cork Factories. Old friend of [[Linnea Elfeklint]].

## Details
- Very prominent in Stockholm society
- Owner of Lysander Cork Factories — an international company; very rich
- Does not have the Sight
- Occult enthusiast: used to hold séances and parties for spiritualists and occultists
- Has a large occult library and a significant collection of occult items
- Believes Vaesen aren't necessarily evil — they are victims of circumstance
- Linnea never got a bad vibe from him
- Contacted Linnea after decades of silence to request help with the murder at the [[Royal Opera]]
- Wants the party to serve as special advisers to the investigation

## Relationships
- [[Linnea Elfeklint]] — old friend; hadn't spoken in decades before sending the letter
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] — close friend; Police Commissioner of Stockholm
- [[Karl-Axel Trygg]] — coachman and valet
- [[Sigrid Holgersdotter]] — on good terms with her due to his wealth

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — client; his letter to Linnea kicked off the adventure; met the party at Stockholm station; covering their accommodations at the [[Golden Peace]]
