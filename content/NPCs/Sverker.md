# Sverker

## Overview
Deceased tenor at the [[Royal Opera]] in Stockholm. Murdered mid-performance. Central victim of Adventure 3.

## Details
- Throat was ripped open while singing a high note during a performance
- Was practicing outside [[St. James Church]] before the murder — sensed something watching him from the shadows
- The note he was singing was slightly out of his range, but he liked to challenge himself
- Was singing *La Navarraise* (French opera)
- Did not have the Sight
- Did not owe money, gamble, or use opium — "loved by all"
- Loved himself most; second was his wife
- His ghost is content where he is — not interested in passing on quickly

## Séance (conducted by party at Castle Gyllencreutz)
- Remembered sensing something watching from the shadows near St. James Church
- Throat was ripped open when he hit the note
- Doesn't know what was watching him or who would want to hurt him
- Described St. James Church as elegantly constructed with great reverb — you can harmonize with yourself

## Relationships
- Wife — unnamed; second most loved person in his life
- [[Erik Johan Stagnelius]] — close friend; spent many nights together at the [[Golden Peace]] after performances
- [[Rufus Holberg]] — understudy; strained relationship over the role dispute in *A Masked Ball*
- [[Mortimer Cronstedt]] — conductor; had decided to replace Sverker with Rufus before both their deaths
