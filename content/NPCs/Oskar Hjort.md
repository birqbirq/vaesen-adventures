# Oskar Hjort

## Overview
A [[Revenant]] — the ghost haunting the [[Witch Cat Inn]]. A playwright who was murdered and whose bones were hidden in the root cellar. Resolved in Adventure 2.

## Details
- Was a well-known playwright who traveled the world putting on plays
- Eventually started seeing things (had the Sight?) and ended up penniless and homeless
- Was "rescued" by [[Albert Wredenhielm|Albert]], a senior [[The Society|Society]] member — but Albert had him killed
- Murdered by [[Pyri Harjula]] at Albert's behest; his bones were hidden in the root cellar of the Witch Cat Inn
- His ghost haunted the inn, causing nightmares in guests (they dreamed of being him and having their throat slit)
- Visited [[Sophia]] nightly, helping her build a shadow theater and write a play telling his story
- Also knew [[Nora]] (Sophia's mother) and encouraged her interest in theater
- Was angry about Albert and about Pyri; his ghost possessed Sami (Pyri's grandson) during the investigation
- Whispers heard at the root cellar: *"albert my love" / "my right to decline" / "you were my everything. You let Pyri kill me"* — suggesting a romantic relationship with Albert
- Was laid to rest when the party found his bones and buried them in consecrated ground (carried over the cemetery wall, not through the gate)

## Status
At rest. Bones buried in consecrated ground near the Witch Cat Inn.

## Relationships
- [[Albert Wredenhielm]] — had a romantic relationship; Albert ordered his murder
- [[Pyri Harjula]] — murdered him on Albert's orders
- [[Sophia]] — visited her nightly as a ghost; guided her to tell his story
- [[Nora]] — knew her; encouraged her love of theater
- [[Sami Harjula]] — possessed him during the investigation (Sami is Pyri's grandson)

## Adventure Appearances
