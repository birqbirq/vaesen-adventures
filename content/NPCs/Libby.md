**Type:** [[Selkie]] (vaesen)

## Description

The tall one and apparent leader of a group of three selkies encountered at the Hamburg train station. Speaks with a Scottish accent. Measured, warm, and perceptive — she noted that Killian seemed like good folk.

## Known Details

- Has been in Hamburg for several years, learning about the region
- Searching for her missing sister **Fern**, last believed near Warsaw
- Her companions are [[Iona]] and a [[Selkie|Sophia]] (not to be confused with [[Sophia]] of the Witch Cat Inn)
- Non-hostile; made friendly contact with the party
- Has the castle phone number ([[Castle Gyllencreutz]]) — may reach out for help

## Relationships

- [[Killian]] — spoke with directly; primary contact with the party
- Iona, Sophia — her companions/sisters
- Fern — missing sister; party offered to help find her after Prague
