# Sigrid Holgersdotter

## Overview
Archivist at the [[National Archives]] in Stockholm. Sharp-featured, precise, and blunt — she does not suffer fools. A useful research resource for historical texts and unusual objects.

## Physical Description
- Sharp cheekbones, pointed chin, precise bearing

## Details
- Works at the [[National Archives]] in Stockholm
- Not an occultist, but extremely knowledgeable — can locate relevant historical texts and point to unusual items held in the collection
- Dislikes people and expects professionalism; not warm but not actively obstructive if you are respectful
- [[August T. Lysander]] is on her good side because of his wealth — money reportedly goes a long way with her
- The party interacted with her on **October 16, 1897** — she was immediately aware they still had the silver knife on loan, and reminded them repeatedly it is one of the Archives' most valuable pieces

## Information She Has Provided
- **Murder geography:** The serial murders are mostly clustered on the central island of Stockholm; only two outliers (the [[Royal Opera]] and the Stockholm church) are to the north. The south, where crime statistics are highest, has seen none — unusual for attacks supposedly targeting criminals.
- **Historical precedents:** No documented local precedents for animal-tearing murders. She referenced Viking berserker legends (bears/wolves in battle) and word-of-mouth accounts from the **Finno-Russian wars ~20 years ago** (Swedish-Finnish soldiers returned with stories they couldn't explain — she attributes this to battlefield hallucination but cannot rule it out).
- **Silver supply:** Two regional silver mines have agents/storefronts in Stockholm. She directed the party to **Sala Jewelers**, near the [[Golden Peace]], as a known silver dealer.

## Relationships
- [[August T. Lysander]] — on good terms due to his wealth
