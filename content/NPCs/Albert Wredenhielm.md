# Albert Wredenhielm

## Overview
Senior member of [[The Society]]. One of the three survivors of the Hulu fire. Ordered the murder of [[Oskar Hjort]].

## Details
- Was one of the highest-ranking members of the Society — not the leader, but very experienced
- Came from Finland, where he led the Society's headquarters before it burned down in the Hulu fire
- Had a romantic relationship with [[Oskar Hjort|Oskar]] ("albert my love" / "you were my everything")
- Ordered [[Pyri Harjula]] to murder Oskar; Oskar's ghost whispered *"you let Pyri kill me"*
- One of three survivors of the Hulu fire who met at the [[Witch Cat Inn]]:
  - Baroness Katja Kokola
  - Professor Albert Wredenhielm *(himself)*
  - Countess Hilma of Thulenstierna
- Referenced in [[Oskar Hjort|Oskar]]'s ghost play as the man who "rescued" Oskar from homelessness before having him killed

## Status
Presumably deceased (events took place ~50 years before the campaign). No confirmed current status.

## Relationships
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — romantic relationship; ordered his murder
- [[Pyri Harjula]] — fellow Society survivor; gave him the order to kill Oskar
- [[The Society]] — senior member, Finland HQ leader

## Adventure Appearances
- [[Adventures/2. Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — referenced throughout; central to the backstory of Oskar's murder
