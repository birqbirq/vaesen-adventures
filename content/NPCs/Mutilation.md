# Mutilation (the Beggar)

## Overview
An unnamed beggar encountered outside the [[Golden Peace]]. One of the few surviving victims of the Whitehill limb transplant operation.

## Appearance
- Right hand is visibly wrong — ulcerated, pale, putrid-smelling, clearly not his own
- Ragged, begging for coin on the streets near the Golden Peace

## Details
- Was paid to undergo the limb transplant procedure
- Most others he knew who went through the same procedure have since died
- Cannot describe the perpetrator well: blonde, clean-shaven, "very Swedish"
- Does not remember where he was taken for the procedure
- Pointed the party toward the **Locking Bridge** as the route to Whitehill
- Accepted [[Abraham]]'s offer to be taken to the [[Seraphim Hospital]] for treatment — party put it on [[August T. Lysander|Lysander]]'s tab
- Would recognize the perpetrator if he saw him again
- **Not a Vaesen**

## Relationships
- [[Abraham]] — offered him coin and hospital care in exchange for information

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — encountered twice; provided key info on the Whitehill transplant operation; escorted to [[Seraphim Hospital]]; **died during treatment** (6/2/26) — hand too far gone from rejection/infection
