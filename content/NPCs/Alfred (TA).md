# Alfred (TA)

## Overview
Teacher's assistant to [[Dr. Djerf]] at Uppsala University. Attempted to steal a valuable first-edition book from [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]'s library.

## Details
- Accompanied Dr. Djerf to the castle under the cover of academic research
- Attempted to pocket a first-edition folklore text — beautifully leather-bound, in immaculate condition, very valuable
- The book's content was folklore, not occult — suggesting the theft was for monetary value, not research
- [[Abraham]] caught him in the act (Vigilance/Logic roll, two successes) but let it play out until they were leaving
- When confronted with Abraham's walking stick, returned the book without further incident
- Declined the offer to stay and read the book under supervision — left quickly with Dr. Djerf

## Relationships
- [[Dr. Djerf]] — employer; possibly complicit in the theft attempt

## Adventure Appearances
- [[4 - Carpathia]] — attempted book theft caught by [[Abraham]]
