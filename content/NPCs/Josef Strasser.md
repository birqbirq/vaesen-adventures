# Josef Strasser

## Overview
Warden of [[Brygidki Prison]] in [[Lemberg]], Galicia. Sent a letter to the Society requesting help with mysterious deaths among his prisoners.

## Details
- Describes himself as not an expert in the supernatural
- Believes in the security of his prison, which makes the deaths all the more unsettling
- The deaths have been gruesome and numerous — several prisoners ill, some dead
- Prohibits a long list of items from being brought inside the prison (weapons, sharp objects, tobacco, alcohol, money, medicine, lighters)
- Emphasizes urgency: "lives are endangered, time is of the essence"

## Relationships
- [[Brygidki Prison]] — the institution he wardens

## Adventure Appearances
- [[4 - Carpathia]] — sent the letter that prompted the party to travel to Galicia
