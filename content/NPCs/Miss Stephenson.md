# Miss Stephenson

## Overview
Housekeeper for [[Coroner Volk|Coroner Esther Volk]]. An older woman who was present during the final confrontation in Volk's apartment. Touched by the supernatural events of October 16, 1897.

## Appearance
- Older woman; manner suggests long domestic service
- Was in a frantic, distressed state when the party arrived at Volk's apartment

## Details
- Served as [[Coroner Volk|Esther Volk]]'s housekeeper
- Had been waiting in a panic — Volk had locked herself in the basement two days prior and Miss Stephenson had no key to the basement door
- She had been hearing occasional **glassware clanking** from the basement but no screaming
- Did not know about Volk's true nature as a werewolf
- Clearly cared for Volk — used her first name when speaking about her
- **Fainted from screaming** when [[Dr. Svardh]] appeared in werewolf form; [[Gabriel Utterson]] tended to her
- Was unconscious for the combat; unharmed

## Potential
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] has arranged for the deaths to be covered up; Miss Stephenson will have questions
- Heather noted she could potentially be recruited to [[Castle Gyllencreutz]] — she and the party have a shared traumatic experience
- No family of her own mentioned; newly without her employer and likely without a position
- Has some knowledge of Volk's habits and daily routines, though not her supernatural secret

## Relationships
- [[Coroner Volk]] — her employer; deceased
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] — the official who responded to the scene; she knows he was present

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — present at Volk's apartment, Oct 16, 1897; fainted during the confrontation; tended by Utterson
