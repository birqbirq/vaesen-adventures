# Professor Liebholz

## Overview
A German performer at [[Bern's Salon]] in Stockholm, presenting an evening of spirits and ghostly visions. Confirmed to be a genuine medium — not a Thursday's child, but has cultivated the ability through lifelong study. Is withholding something from the party.

## Appearance
- Suit and cape, walking stick, precise posture
- Carries a mirror onstage as his focal vessel for spirit contact

## Details
- Running "Spirits and Ghostly Visions" at [[Bern's Salon]]; show runs Sunday and Monday evenings
- Admission: one pound general, three pounds for a numbered seat
- **Not a Thursday's child** — confirmed directly; his ability is cultivated through practice and study, not a birthright
- Summons spirits through a reflective surface (mirror); the spirit appears in the glass
- Primary focus: connecting the living with deceased loved ones; avoids criminal cases, finding them too heavy to bear
- Has heard of unusual paranormal activity in Stockholm through his circles — but said "nothing concerning"
- **Both [[Killian]] and [[Niklas Natt|Niklas]] sensed he was holding something back** during their meeting
- When pressed, he calmly asked the party to leave; Killian took a mental injury (Angry) from the failed attempt to elicit the withheld information
- Has not been involved with any of the murder victims, per his own account

## Demonstrated Abilities
- Successfully summoned the spirit of [[Friedrich Balm]] (the murdered organist) on request, in a mirror onstage
- Friedrich answered a specific, private question correctly — confirming the contact was genuine, not theatrical

## Relationship to Murders
- Aware of the killings from rumors and the papers, but has kept his distance
- What he is withholding may be relevant to the case — nature unknown

## Status
**Requires follow-up** — the party has not gotten everything he knows. He is not hostile, but will not be pushed.

## Relationships
- [[August Strindberg]] — fellow regular at [[Bern's Salon]]; familiar with his work but not close

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — noted from the City Gazette; party met him 6/23/26; confirmed as genuine medium; withholding something; Killian took mental injury (Angry) during the encounter; party returned to the bar
