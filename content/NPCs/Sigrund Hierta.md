# Sigrund Hierta

## Overview
Journalist based in Uppsala. Ally and contact to the party, though initially coercive.

## Details
- Met the party at the Burger & Baker inn while taking notes
- Offered to keep the party's activities quiet in exchange for first crack at the story
- Has connections with publishers; offered to help Abraham get his novel published
- Threatened to expose the party if they didn't cooperate
- Has friends in the town government and police
- Has extensive knowledge of the castle's history and the disappearances (sourced largely from Lotte the barkeep)
- Believes in understanding what he writes about; made it his job to know what's really going on

## Information Sigrund Provided (Adv 1)
- 3 people had ventured to the castle and not returned
- 2 families (Djerf and Thorsson) had members disappear at the castle
- The castle was originally a beacon for those with the Sight, and a sign of devil worship for others
- Rumors that beer was being brewed at the castle (Ask Small Ale)

- [[Erik Johan Stagnelius]] — childhood friend from Uppsala; introduced the party to him as a local Stockholm contact
