# Coroner Volk

## Overview
Stockholm city coroner. Works in the basement morgue of the police headquarters on Coyne Street.

## Appearance
- Medium height, fair hair, piercing green eyes
- Deep old scar running from her right ear down to her jaw

## Details
- Sharp, observant, and more forthcoming than [[Gabriel Utterson]]
- Confirmed that [[Mortimer Cronstedt]]'s death was deliberate murder — opium poisoning, not turtle soup
- Determined that [[Sverker]] von Essen's throat wound was caused by one decisive, immensely powerful grab — consistent with a large canine or animal, not a human
- All murder victims died from blood loss via violent tearing wounds (outside-in)
- Identified the Whitehill "disease" as forced limb transplants — not a disease at all
- Frustrated that the strangled young women found around the city are being ignored by the police
- Believes the transplant surgeries are being performed by someone outside the [[Seraphim Hospital]]

## Relationships
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] — works under his jurisdiction; more candid than he is

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — interviewed in the morgue; provided key forensic details on all victims
