# Coroner Volk

## Overview
Stockholm city coroner. Works in the basement morgue of the police headquarters on Coyne Street. **Deceased — killed during the resolution of the S.o.a.K. mystery (Oct 16, 1897).**

## Appearance
- Medium height, fair hair, piercing green eyes
- Deep old scar running from her right ear down to her jaw

## Details
- Sharp, observant, and more forthcoming than [[Gabriel Utterson]]
- Confirmed that [[Mortimer Cronstedt]]'s death was deliberate murder — opium poisoning, not turtle soup
- Determined that [[Sverker]] von Essen's throat wound was caused by one decisive, immensely powerful grab — consistent with a large canine or animal, not a human
- All murder victims died from blood loss via violent tearing wounds (outside-in)
- Identified the Whitehill "disease" as forced limb transplants — not a disease at all
- Frustrated that the strangled young women found around the city are being ignored by the police
- Believes the transplant surgeries are being performed by someone outside the [[Seraphim Hospital]]

## True Identity — Revealed Oct 16, 1897
- **She was a werewolf** — the original serial killer responsible for the S.o.a.K. murders
- Full first name: **Esther**
- Originally from **Finland**; came to Stockholm after the war seeking a simpler life
- Served as a **field medic** during the war — likely how she was cursed (consistent with Archive records of werewolf rumors during the Russo-Finnish War)
- Had been **chaining herself** in her basement laboratory to prevent harming others
- Had been working on a **chemical concoction** to suppress the transformation — it worked for years, then recently stopped
- Had **no close relatives** — no one who could break the curse by calling her human name
- Kept a **pocket watch** engraved with a lieutenant's name (F---e--- — first initial F, contains E, rest illegible) with a photograph of herself and a young man in military fatigues inside
- Asked the party to kill her with her own silver bullets when she revealed herself

## Death
- Shot in the leg with a silver bullet by [[Killian]] during the confrontation in her basement lab (Oct 16, 1897)
- Weakened by the wound and by exhaustion from fighting the chains and her own transformation — expired
- Reverted to human form after death
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] has arranged for the deaths to be covered as "natural causes"

## Relationships
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] — worked under his jurisdiction; he was visibly shaken by her death
- [[Miss Stephenson]] — her housekeeper; an older woman who clearly cared for her
- Unknown Lieutenant (F---e---) — person depicted in the pocket watch photograph; military connection

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — interviewed in the morgue; provided key forensic details on all victims; revealed as the original werewolf serial killer Oct 16, 1897; deceased
