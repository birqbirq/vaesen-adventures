# Pyri Harjula

## Overview
Deceased innkeeper of the [[Witch Cat Inn]]. Grandfather of [[Sami Harjula]]. Murdered [[Oskar Hjort]] on orders from [[Albert Wredenhielm]].

## Details
- Was a member of [[The Society]] (joined after it was renamed from the [[Order of Artemis]])
- Had the Sight
- Rebuilt the Witch Cat Inn after the Hulu fire
- Murdered Oskar Hjort at Albert's behest; hid the bones in the root cellar
- Died of old age and stress from running the inn
- His ghost kept watch over his grandson Sami running the inn
- Message left for Sami via séance: *"Let him know I'm proud of him"*
- Believed Vaesen had room for good and that the evil Vaesen problem could be solved
- The 3 surviving members of the Hulu fire met at his inn: Baroness Katja Kokola, Professor Albert Wredenhielm, Countess Hilma of Thulenstierna

## Séance (conducted by party en route to the inn)
- Confirmed no secret passages or rooms in the inn
- Said the Hulu fire was caused by Giants from Finland (matriarch targeted hunters and miners)
- Said the Rosenbergs left the Society after the fire and created a new group led by Johanna Lampinen
- Warned the party: *"Don't go in the cellar"* — wouldn't elaborate

## Relationships
- [[Sami Harjula]] — grandson; Pyri watches over him
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — murdered him on Albert's orders
- [[Albert Wredenhielm]] — fellow Society survivor of the Hulu fire; gave the order to kill Oskar
- [[The Society]] — member

## Adventure Appearances
- [[2 - Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — contacted via séance; his actions (murdering Oskar) were the root cause of the haunting
