# Nora

## Overview
Deceased wife of [[Sami Harjula]]. Her spirit lingered at the [[Witch Cat Inn]].

## Details
- Wanted to be an actor; was encouraged in this by [[Oskar Hjort|Oskar]]'s ghost, who talked her into following a traveling troupe of performers
- Was haunted by a ghost while alive, but nobody believed her — they thought she was being dramatic
- Her spirit caused a supernatural event during the investigation: everyone in the inn fell asleep, lamps and fire turned blue and gave no heat, temperature dropped to freezing
- Sami recognized the event as Nora's doing and said they needed to get out
- Her conservatory in the back garden is now rubble
- Her journal was given to the party by Sami after Oskar was laid to rest

## Relationships
- [[Sami Harjula]] — husband
- [[Sophia]] — daughter
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — knew him; his ghost encouraged her love of theater
