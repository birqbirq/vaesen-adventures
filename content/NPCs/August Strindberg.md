# August Strindberg

## Overview
Prominent Swedish writer and political radical. Regular fixture at [[Bern's Salon]] in Stockholm. Fan of [[Abraham]]'s published work. Available as a contact.

## Appearance
- Life of the party at his corner table; animated, gestures broadly when speaking
- Surrounded by a circle of admirers and fellow intellectuals

## Details
- One of the most prominent Swedish writers of the age
- Takes most of his inspiration from [[Bern's Salon]] itself — it is his primary haunt
- Work tends toward the political and radical rather than the supernatural
- Has no occult knowledge and does not believe in werewolves or vaesen
- Politically charged: deeply sympathetic to the poor and homeless; contemptuous of the English; frustrated with the government and the royal family
- Recognized [[Abraham]] as the author of works he admires; offered to proof Abraham's forthcoming novel (thriller with supernatural elements)
- Provided his address so Abraham can send him a copy when it's published

## Relationship to the Investigation
- Knows Stockholm social circles well but has no direct information about the murders
- May be useful for introductions to other Stockholm figures if approached

## Relationships
- [[Abraham]] — new contact; fan of his work
- [[Professor Liebholz]] — fellow regular at [[Bern's Salon]]; acquainted but not close
