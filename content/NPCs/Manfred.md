# Manfred

## Overview
[[Linnea Elfeklint|Linnea]]'s former fiancé. Villain of Adventure 1. Transformed into a bat-winged creature through occult means.

## Details
- Ran a brewery called Ask Small Ale (also brewed at [[Castle Gyllencreutz]])
- Linnea feared he only wanted her for the castle
- Used the castle's occult temple to trap the souls of people with the Sight in a green-glowing fermentation vat
- Transformed into a bat-winged creature with claws; commanded ghost lights
- Was defeated when [[Niklas Natt|Niklas]] took the castle deed from the cabinet door — this broke Manfred's power and his ghostly spirit fled the castle
- Had been sending luring notes to people with the Sight ("come to the castle and I'll help your dreams come true")
- Had forbidden cult texts in the library: *Carpathian Séance*, *The Haunted Hand of Lucas*, *The Gospel of The Dead*

## Relationships
- [[Linnea Elfeklint]] — former fiancée; used her to gain access to the castle
- Victims — trapped souls of multiple people with the Sight in the vat (at least 3 ghosts)

## Status
Defeated. Ghostly spirit fled the castle after the deed was taken. Presumed gone.

## Adventure Appearances
- [[1 - Intro Session|Intro Session]] — primary villain; defeated at the end of the adventure
