# Sophia

## Overview
Daughter of [[Sami Harjula]], innkeeper of the [[Witch Cat Inn]]. Was visited nightly by [[Oskar Hjort|Oskar]]'s ghost and built a shadow theater to tell his story.

## Details
- Has the Sight
- Built a shadow theater in the inn's attic
- Was visited every night by Oskar's ghost, who helped her write the play, build the theater, and guided what she said during performances
- During the play, became possessed — her voice changed, posture shifted, and she channeled Oskar's story
- Came back to herself after the ghost's dramatic appearance; didn't remember being possessed
- Oskar also knew her mother [[Nora]] and encouraged Nora's interest in theater; talked her into following a traveling troupe of performers
- Oskar got angry when talking about [[Albert Wredenhielm|Albert]] and [[Pyri Harjula|Pyri]] during his visits

## Relationships
- [[Sami Harjula]] — father
- [[Nora]] — deceased mother
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — was visited by his ghost nightly; he guided her to tell his story

## Adventure Appearances
- [[2 - Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — performed the shadow play; key to uncovering Oskar's story
