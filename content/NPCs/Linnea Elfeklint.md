# Linnea Elfeklint

## Overview
The last surviving member of [[The Society]]. Elderly woman living at the Uppsala asylum. Mentor figure to the party.

## Details
- Has the Sight; describes herself as "like us"
- Was the sole owner of [[Castle Gyllencreutz]], left to her roughly 20 years before the campaign
- Was chased out of the castle by a green-glowing vaesen ~2.5 months before the party arrived
- Her fiancé [[Manfred]] ran a brewery (Ask Small Ale) and she fears he only wanted her for the castle
- Has an intuitive sense for bad intentions — can usually tell when something feels wrong
- Lives in room 120 at the Uppsala asylum; staff treat her visits from the party as normal
- Gave the party the castle deed after they resolved the Manfred situation, asking them to rebuild the Society

## Relationships
- [[Manfred]] — former fiancé, turned villain
- [[Algot Frisk]] — former castle butler, now serves the party
- [[August T. Lysander]] — old friend in Stockholm; hasn't spoken to him in decades
- [[Olaus Klint]] — knows of his family's falling out with the Society; no bad feelings toward him personally
