# Erik Johan Stagnelius

## Overview
Poet and actor based in Stockholm. Close friend of [[Sverker]] von Essen. Permanent resident at the [[Golden Peace]] hotel.

## Details
- Originally from Uppsala; has known [[Sigrund Hierta]] since childhood
- Deeply embedded in the Stockholm arts and theater scene
- Was one of [[Sverker]]'s closest friends — spent many nights at the Golden Peace tavern after performances
- Short, blunt hair, pointed nose; loud and boisterous; recites poetry in the tavern
- Drinks heavily
- Does not believe in the supernatural — only uses it as poetic material
- Recognized Abraham's published poetry on sight and was impressed

## Information Provided (Adv 3)
- The opera company (conductor and staff) had decided [[Sverker]] had run his course in *A Masked Ball* and planned to replace him with his understudy [[Rufus Holberg]]
- Sverker was furious and refused to step aside — this may have made him a target
- The conductor [[Mortimer Cronstedt]] is dead, so couldn't have done it
- Erik considers [[Rufus Holberg]] the prime suspect, though he doesn't know him well
- Rufus occasionally comes to the Golden Peace but mostly stays away

## Relationships
- [[Sverker]] — close friend; deeply saddened by his death
- [[Sigrund Hierta]] — childhood friend from Uppsala
- [[Abraham]] — new acquaintance; impressed by his poetry; wants a copy of his novel
