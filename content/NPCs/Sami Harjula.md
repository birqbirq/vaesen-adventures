# Sami Harjula

## Overview
Innkeeper of the [[Witch Cat Inn]]. Grandson of [[Pyri Harjula]]. Does not have the Sight.

## Details
- Has beautiful eyes
- Was not having the nightmares that other inn guests experienced
- Does not have the Sight
- Was upset about his daughter [[Sophia]]'s shadow theater play and yelled at her about it
- Was possessed/sleepwalked during the investigation — walked to the stable saying "I have to pay" and "I killed someone" (he was being punished for his grandfather Pyri's actions)
- After the haunting was resolved, was told his grandfather was a bad person; said he needed to shut the tavern and think
- Gave the party [[Nora's Journal|Nora's journal]] after Oskar was laid to rest
- His grandfather Pyri's ghost watches over him and is proud of him

## Relationships
- [[Pyri Harjula]] — grandfather; Pyri's ghost watches over him
- [[Sophia]] — daughter
- [[Nora]] — deceased wife; had a conservatory in the back garden (now rubble)
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — Oskar's ghost haunted the inn and possessed Sami

## Adventure Appearances
- [[2 - Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — innkeeper; central to the haunting investigation
