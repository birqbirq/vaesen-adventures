# Gabriel Utterson

## Overview
Police Commissioner of Stockholm. Close friend of [[August T. Lysander]].

## Details
- Head of Stockholm's newly established police force, which is struggling
- Has shared the details of [[Sverker]] von Essen's murder with [[August T. Lysander]] — gruesome enough that Lysander believes no human could be responsible
- Issued a public statement in the City Gazette promising justice for Sverker's murder; no suspects identified
- Headquarters on Coyne Street — the first building on the island as you cross the bridge into the Town Between Bridges
- [[August T. Lysander]] recommends the party speak with him

## Relationships
- [[August T. Lysander]] — close friend; shared case details with him
