# Gabriel Utterson

## Overview
Police Commissioner of Stockholm. Close friend of [[August T. Lysander]].

## Appearance
- Older man, sharp hazel eyes, strong features, stern expression

## Details
- Head of Stockholm's newly established police force, which is struggling
- Has shared the details of [[Sverker]] von Essen's murder with [[August T. Lysander]] — gruesome enough that Lysander believes no human could be responsible
- Issued a public statement in the City Gazette promising justice for Sverker's murder; no suspects identified
- Headquarters on Coyne Street — the first building on the island as you cross the bridge into the [[Town Between Bridges]]
- Skeptical of supernatural involvement — believes it's a "Jack the Ripper type" human killer
- Confirmed the murder pattern: all victims died from violent tearing wounds; prior victims were criminals in Whitehill
- Provided the party with a list of murder dates — another killing may be imminent
- **Covering up [[Mortimer Cronstedt]]'s murder** as a turtle soup accident to prevent public panic amid political unrest
- [[Rufus Holberg]] has an alibi (was at [[Bern's Salon]] with colleagues) — Utterson does not consider him a suspect
- Has patrols in [[Whitehill]] but residents won't cooperate

## Relationships
- [[August T. Lysander]] — close friend; shared case details with him
- [[Coroner Volk]] — works in the basement morgue of his headquarters

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — quoted in the City Gazette; recommended as a contact by Lysander; interviewed by the party 4/14/26; updated by the party on [[Seraphim Hospital]] findings 6/2/26 — dismissed the coroner's assessment, going with the hospital's "disease" explanation; responded to fountain attack 7/7/26; personally witnessed [[Dr. Svardh]] transform from werewolf to human form after capture; has [[Dr. Svardh]] in police custody; actively working with the party; sourcing more silver weapons
