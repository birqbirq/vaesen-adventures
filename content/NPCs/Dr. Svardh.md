# Dr. Jens Svardh

## Overview
Senior physician and research director at [[Seraphim Hospital]]. Confirmed as the perpetrator of the [[Whitehill]] limb transplant surgeries. Attacked by the werewolf vaesen, survived, and subsequently transformed into a werewolf himself. Captured and currently in police custody.

## Appearance
- Tall, well-dressed, clean-shaven — described by Whitehill residents as "the fancy guy," visibly out of place in the slums
- Physical appearance after capture: back wounds reopened, bullet wounds, slash from silver knife; badly injured

## Details
- Most well-renowned doctor at [[Seraphim Hospital]]; present since the hospital opened
- Trained and oversaw medical research across a wide range of areas
- Supervised junior doctors including [[Dr. Tyko Gregorius]]
- Regularly visited [[Whitehill]] slums — ostensibly to treat patients, actually to conduct transplant experiments
- **Confirmed as "the fancy guy"** — the well-dressed surgeon who paid Whitehill residents to take their limbs and replace them with cadaver limbs
- His leather satchel, found at the fountain scene, contained **surgical instruments** and a notebook titled *"Transplantation of Extremities from Cadavers — Experimental Series 3A"* — direct evidence of his guilt
- The notebook confirms the transplant work was systematic and deliberate experimentation, not treatment

## The Fountain Attack (Oct 15, 1897)
- Found face-down in a fountain in a merchant square near a cathedral, bleeding heavily — attacked by the werewolf vaesen
- His wife was with him; she was knocked unconscious and remembered only a growl and **large green glowing eyes**
- [[Killian]] applied field medicine (with [[Abraham]]'s assistance) and stabilized him; the party's intervention likely saved his life
- Taken by ambulance to [[Seraphim Hospital]]

## Transformation (Oct 15, 1897)
- At the hospital, broke through a second-floor window from the inside out, escaping into the city
- Confirmed to have **transformed into a werewolf** — the bite/wounds from the vaesen appear to have triggered rapid transformation
- Two distinct howls were heard during the subsequent chase, indicating **at least two werewolves** are active in Stockholm

## Capture (Oct 16, 1897)
- Tracked through pre-dawn Stockholm using screams; cornered near the [[Golden Peace]] hotel
- **[[Niklas Natt|Niklas]]** charged in with the silver knife and struck first (2 damage); were-Svardh bit Niklas (1 physical injury: Wounded)
- **[[Killian]]** shot with a silver bullet (2 damage); were-Svardh began fleeing
- Niklas threw the silver knife at the retreating werewolf — **knocked unconscious**
- Transformed back to human form while unconscious; manacled by [[Gabriel Utterson|Commissioner Utterson]]
- Currently in **police custody**, badly injured; condition unknown
