# Dr. Svardh

## Overview
Senior physician and research director at [[Seraphim Hospital]]. Has been at the hospital since it opened. A suspect of interest in the Whitehill limb transplant investigation.

## Appearance

- Has been there since the hospital opened
- Trains and oversees medical research across a wide range of areas
- Oversees junior doctors including [[Dr. Tyko Gregorius]]

- On shift Sunday morning (the morning after the 6/2/26 session)
- [[Coroner Volk]] does not believe the hospital itself is responsible for the transplants, but she left open the possibility of an individual acting on their own
- Has access to all necessary surgical supplies
