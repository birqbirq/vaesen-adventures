# Rufus Holberg

## Overview
Understudy tenor at the [[Royal Opera]] in Stockholm. Took over [[Sverker]] von Essen's lead role in Verdi's *A Masked Ball* after Sverker's murder.

## Details
- Was [[Sverker]]'s understudy; the opera company had already planned to replace Sverker with him before the murder
- Sverker was furious about being replaced and refused to step aside
- [[Erik Johan Stagnelius]] considers him the prime suspect in Sverker's murder, though the police have not named any suspects
- Occasionally visits the [[Golden Peace]] tavern but mostly stays away
- Erik doesn't know him well

## Relationships
- [[Sverker]] — predecessor; their relationship was strained over the role dispute

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — named as a suspect by Erik Johan Stagnelius
