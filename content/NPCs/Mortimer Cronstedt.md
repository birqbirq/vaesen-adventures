# Mortimer Cronstedt

## Overview
Deceased conductor of the [[Royal Opera]] in Stockholm. Died approximately six weeks before the party's arrival.

## Details
- Well-known in high society circles
- **Officially:** died from turtle soup / botulism at the [[Golden Peace]] tavern
- **Actually:** deliberately poisoned with a massive opium overdose — confirmed murder by [[Coroner Volk]]
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] is covering up the true cause of death to prevent public panic amid political unrest
- Had decided, along with the opera staff, to replace [[Sverker]] von Essen with understudy [[Rufus Holberg]] before Sverker's murder
- Niklas wants to investigate whether anyone took out life insurance on him

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — death reported in the City Gazette; confirmed murder by Coroner Volk on 4/14/26
