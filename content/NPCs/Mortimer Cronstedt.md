# Mortimer Cronstedt

## Overview
Deceased conductor of the [[Royal Opera]] in Stockholm. Died approximately six weeks before the party's arrival.

## Details
- Well-known in high society circles
- Died after consuming turtle soup at the [[Golden Peace]] tavern — suspected botulism
- As far as [[August T. Lysander]] knows, only one person died from the soup
- Had decided, along with the opera staff, to replace [[Sverker]] von Essen with understudy [[Rufus Holberg]] before Sverker's murder
- Niklas wants to investigate whether anyone took out life insurance on him

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — death reported in the City Gazette; possible connection to the opera murders
