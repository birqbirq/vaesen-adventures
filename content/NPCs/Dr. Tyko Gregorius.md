# Dr. Tyko Gregorius

## Overview
Junior doctor at [[Seraphim Hospital]]. Treated the party's beggar contact ([[Mutilation]]) and provided key intelligence on the Whitehill transplant operation.

## Appearance
- Large, dark-haired
- Doesn't look like a typical doctor

## Details
- Has been at [[Seraphim Hospital]] for 3 years (as of the session; started straight out of medical school)
- Calls the Whitehill deaths "the pauper's disease"
- Most Whitehill victims arrive already beyond saving — go directly to the morgue
- Has not gone to Whitehill himself to treat patients
- Trained under and overseen by [[Dr. Svardh]], the hospital's most senior and respected physician
- Does not believe in magic — only science
- Assessed the transplant surgery as technically precise and well-executed; the problem is biological rejection, not poor technique
- Theorizes the perpetrator is someone without formal credentials: a former army medic, a self-taught practitioner, or someone from another institution
- Warned the party: look for death-smell, large quantities of trash/waste bags, decay odor, and thick black smoke (incineration) to find the surgery location
- Key supplies the perpetrator would need: opium, lye soap, aspirin, cocaine, and other pain relievers — only sold through hospitals, so an inside source may be involved

## Relationships
- [[Dr. Svardh]] — mentor and supervisor
- [[Mutilation]] — treated (unsuccessfully); patient died
