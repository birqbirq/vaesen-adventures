# Dr. Djerf

## Overview
A philosopher with a doctorate from Uppsala University. Visited [[Castle Gyllencreutz]] with his assistant [[Alfred (TA)]] claiming to want access to the library for occult research.

## Appearance
- Clearly nervous during the visit — sweating, not fully trustworthy

## Details
- Has a genuine personal reason for investigating the occult: **his wife went missing at the castle**
- He's trying to understand what happened to her
- A capable intellectual in his area (philosophy) but surprisingly naive outside it — [[Abraham]] noted he seemed genuinely smart but not broadly clever
- Likely knew his TA Alfred was attempting to steal a book, or at least suspected it
- Genuinely engaged with the occult conversation during the library tour — light bulbs visibly going off by the end
- Declined Abraham's offer to read the first-edition book on site before leaving

## Relationships
- [[Alfred (TA)]] — his teacher's assistant; caught attempting to steal a valuable first edition from the library
- **Wife (missing)** — went missing at [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]; reason he came to the castle

## Status
- [[Algot Frisk]] has been asked to verify his credentials with the university

## Adventure Appearances
- [[4 - Carpathia]] — appeared at the castle requesting library access; Abraham caught his TA stealing
