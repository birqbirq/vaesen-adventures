# Sigrid

## Overview
Archivist at the National Archives in Stockholm. Knowledgeable resource for historical texts and occult objects.

## Details
- Works at the National Archives in Stockholm
- Not an occultist, but very knowledgeable — can point to relevant historical texts and interesting objects
- Dislikes people; difficult to approach
- [[August T. Lysander]] is on her good side because he is wealthy — bribing her is reportedly effective
- Lysander recommends the party seek her out for research assistance

## Relationships
- [[August T. Lysander]] — on good terms with him due to his wealth
