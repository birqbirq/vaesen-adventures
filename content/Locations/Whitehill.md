# Whitehill

## Overview
A slum district on the south side of Stockholm. Epicenter of the mysterious "disease" that is actually a forced limb transplant operation.

## Details
- Located on the south side of the city — far from the [[Seraphim Hospital]] on the north side
- Residents are poor and do not trust the police; patrols get little cooperation
- Victims of the transplant operation are being paid to undergo the procedure
- Most victims die from rejection/infection before they can reach the hospital
- The perpetrator (blonde, clean-shaven, "very Swedish") is operating somewhere in or near this district
- The Locking Bridge is the route from the [[Town Between Bridges]] to Whitehill
- A doctor named **Jens S—** has been coming here to treat victims
