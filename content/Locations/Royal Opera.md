# Royal Opera (Stockholm)

## Overview
The Royal Opera in Stockholm. Site of the murder that kicks off Adventure 3.

## Details
- Tenor [[Sverker]] was killed here — throat ripped open mid-performance while singing a high note
- [[August T. Lysander]] contacted [[Linnea Elfeklint]] about the murder, believing the killer is not human
- The party has been invited to serve as special advisers to the investigation
