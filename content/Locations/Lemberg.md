# Lemberg

## Overview
A city in the western part of Galicia, in the Carpathian region. Also known by its Ukrainian name Lviv (German: Lemberg). Destination for the party's current adventure.

## Details
- Located in western Galicia, near the Carpathian mountains
- Can be reached from Prague via Krakow by train — a two-day journey (roughly)
- Sits near the Ottoman Empire border region on the Mythic Carpathia map
- The German name for what is today Lviv, Ukraine

## Travel Route from Uppsala
Uppsala → Stockholm → Hamburg (overnight) → Prague (overnight, Society contact stop) → Lemberg (arrive ~late Wednesday / Day 4)
