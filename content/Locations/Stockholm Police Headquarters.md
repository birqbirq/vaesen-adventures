# Stockholm Police Headquarters

## Overview
The new Stockholm police headquarters on Coyne Street. The first building on the [[Town Between Bridges]] island as you cross the bridge from the mainland.

## Details
- A newer building overlooking the stream of Stockholm, a few blocks from the [[Golden Peace]]
- Houses [[Gabriel Utterson]]'s office on the third floor (accessible by elevator)
- The basement morgue is where [[Coroner Volk]] works

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.]] — party visited to interview [[Gabriel Utterson]] and [[Coroner Volk]]
