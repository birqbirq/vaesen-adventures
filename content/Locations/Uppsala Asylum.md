# Uppsala Asylum

## Overview
An asylum in Uppsala where [[Linnea Elfeklint]] lives. A safe haven for people with the Sight.

## Details
- Linnea lives in room 120
- Has historically been a gathering place for people with the Sight — several went missing from here around the time Linnea was chased from the castle
- Staff treat the party's visits to Linnea as routine
