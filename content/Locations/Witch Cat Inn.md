# Witch Cat Inn

## Overview
An inn on the road to Sigtuna by Lake Mälar. Run by [[Sami Harjula]]. Site of Adventure 2.

## Details
- Originally destroyed in the Hulu fire; rebuilt by [[Pyri Harjula]] (Sami's grandfather), who had the Sight and was a Society member
- [[Oskar Hjort|Oskar]]'s bones were hidden in the root cellar by Pyri on [[Albert Wredenhielm|Albert]]'s orders
- Guests had been experiencing nightmares for roughly a month before the party arrived — dreaming of being a well-educated man whose throat was slit at the inn
- Has a conservatory in the back garden (now rubble — was [[Nora]]'s)
- Root cellar in the northwest — where Oskar's bones were found; Pyri warned "don't go in the cellar" via séance
- Has an attic where [[Sophia]] built her shadow theater

## Relationships
- [[Sami Harjula]] — current innkeeper
- [[Pyri Harjula]] — rebuilt it; his ghost still watches over it
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — haunted it as a Revenant
- [[Olaus Klint]] — sent the party here to investigate
