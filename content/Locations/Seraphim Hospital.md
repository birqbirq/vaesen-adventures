# Seraphim Hospital

## Overview
Stockholm's main hospital. Located on the north side of the city.

## Details
- The primary medical facility in Stockholm
- A doctor named **Jens S—** (last name unknown, starts with S) has been traveling to the [[Whitehill]] slums to treat "disease" victims
- [[Coroner Volk]] does not believe the hospital is responsible for the Whitehill limb transplants — the equipment would be available here, but she rules it out
- [[Mutilation]] (the beggar) was brought here by the party for treatment of his transplanted hand — covered by [[August T. Lysander]]
- Whitehill is on the south side of the city; the hospital is on the north side — the journey is too far for dying transplant victims to survive
