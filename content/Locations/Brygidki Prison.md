# Brygidki Prison

## Overview
A prison in the city of [[Lemberg]], in the western part of Galicia, in the Carpathian region. The site of mysterious prisoner deaths that prompted Warden [[Josef Strasser]] to contact the Society.

## Details
- Managed by Warden [[Josef Strasser]]
- Several prisoners have fallen ill; some have died gruesome deaths
- The warden believes the deaths are supernatural in origin
- The prison has strict rules about what can be brought inside (no weapons, sharp objects, tobacco, alcohol, money, medicine, or fire-starting materials)

## Prohibited Items
Per the warden's letter, the following are not permitted inside:
- Explosives or weapons of any kind
- Anything sharp (knives, scissors)
- Tobacco or tobacco products
- Alcohol or alcohol-containing food/drinks
- Money or valuables
- Medicine or drugs
- Lighters or matches

## Relationships
- [[Josef Strasser]] — warden
- [[Lemberg]] — the city where it's located
