# St. James Church (Stockholm)

## Overview
A church in Stockholm where [[Sverker]] was practicing before his murder.

## Details
- Elegantly constructed; has excellent acoustics — great reverb, you can harmonize with yourself
- Sverker was practicing outside the church when he sensed something watching him from the shadows
- His throat was ripped open when he hit a high note shortly after

## Adventure Appearances
- [[3 - S.o.a.K.|New Adventure]] — referenced in Sverker's séance as the location where he was stalked before his death
