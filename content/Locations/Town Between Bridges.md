# Town Between Bridges

## Overview
The central island district of Stockholm. The party's primary area of operation for Adventure 3.

## Details
- Accessed by crossing a bridge from the mainland
- The police headquarters on Coyne Street is the first building on the island as you cross the bridge
- The [[Golden Peace]] hotel is on the southeast side of the island
- The National Archives are on a neighboring island
