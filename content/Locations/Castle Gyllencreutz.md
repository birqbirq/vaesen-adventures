# Castle Gyllencreutz

## Overview
The party's headquarters, located in Uppsala. Former base of [[The Society]]. Now being rebuilt by the party.

## Details
- Large castle with multiple floors, a basement, and an attic crawlspace
- Was left to [[Linnea Elfeklint]] roughly 20 years before the campaign
- Linnea was chased out ~2.5 months before the party arrived by [[Manfred]]'s green-glowing vaesen
- Was in disrepair when the party arrived — dust, overgrown grounds, animal activity, structural damage
- Metal gate flanked by gryphons at the entrance
- [[Manfred]] had been brewing Ask Small Ale in the kitchen and basement

## Known Rooms
- **Library** — cleaned recently; books on vaesen, science, philosophy; contains forbidden cult texts
- **Séance Parlor** — cleanest room in the castle; Ouija board, crystal ball; now upgraded by the party
- **Laboratory (3rd floor)** — where Ulf's body was found
- **Occult Temple (basement)** — large green-glowing fermentation vat; where Manfred trapped souls; adjacent occult workshop
- **Infirmary (basement)**, **Cellar Vault**, **Dungeon**, **Shooting Range**
- **Linnea's former bedroom (3rd floor)**

## Upgrades

- Butler [[Algot Frisk]] (free, starting)
