# Vaesen Rules Summary
*Revised Edition — condensed reference*

---

## Core Mechanic

Roll **Attribute + Skill** dice (d6s). Each **6 = 1 success**. Most tests need **1 success**.

- **Challenging** action = 2 successes required
- **Difficult** action = 3 successes required
- **Extra successes** = better result; some skills let you spend extras for special effects
- **Fail?** You can **push the roll** (re-roll all non-sixes) — but you must take a Condition first
- **Passive rolls** (e.g. VIGILANCE for ambush detection) cannot be pushed
- Bonuses from talents, gear, and Advantage all **add together** as extra dice

## Conditions

**3 physical:** Exhausted · Battered · Wounded (Physique/Precision skills)  
**3 mental:** Angry · Frightened · Hopeless (Logic/Empathy skills)

- Each active Condition = **–1 die** to that type's skill rolls (they stack)
- Always roll **at least 1 die** regardless of penalties
- 4th Condition of same type = **Broken** → roll on critical injury table

### Healing Conditions
| Method | Heals | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Rest** (1/day) | 1–2 Conditions | 1 alone; 2 with another person or memento |
| **MEDICINE** (hours, safe) | 3 per success | Physical only; not yourself |
| **INSPIRATION** (hours, safe) | 3 per success | Mental only; willing target |
| **Memento** (rest scene) | 2 Conditions | Once per session |
| **Confession** (Absolution talent) | 3 Conditions | During rest |

## Broken & Critical Injuries

When Broken: can't make successful rolls; roll D66 on crit table.

- **Physical crits:** Afflictions (skill penalties) or Insights (skill bonuses)
- **Mental crits:** same structure; some require treatment within time limit or become chronic/fatal
- **Fatal/Chronic** entries have a time limit — treat with MEDICINE or INSPIRATION before it expires or character dies/is permanently Broken

### Recovery (between mysteries)
Roll **Physique + Precision** dice (physical) or **Logic + Empathy** dice (mental).  
Each success: heal an Affliction **or** make an Insight permanent.  
Fail = Afflictions become permanent and Insights wear off.

## Experience Points (XP)

**Earn:** Answer 8 yes/no questions at session end (1 XP per yes, min 1).

**Spend:** Every **5 XP = 1 Advance**:
- Raise a skill by 1 (max 5)
- Buy any talent (any archetype, no limit on total talents)

**Also:** 1 XP to repair/replace a lost memento.

---

## Development Points (DP)

**Earn:** Answer 8 yes/no questions after completing a mystery (1 DP per yes).

**Spend:** Purchase HQ Upgrades (Facilities, Contacts, Personnel).

> Each Upgrade purchase triggers a threat roll (dice = Upgrade cost; any 6 = GM picks a threat from the list).

---

## Advantage

- Gained once per mystery (during travel to the location)
- **+2 dice** to one skill test; declare before rolling or when pushing
- Lost when returning to Uppsala; next Advantage must be a different skill

## Combat

**Initiative:** Draw cards 1–10; lowest acts first. Cards can be swapped via AGILITY extra successes.

**Actions per round:** 1 slow + 1 fast (or 2 fast)
- **Slow:** Attack, flee, persuade, ritual, treat injury, etc.
- **Fast (reactions):** Dodge (AGILITY), parry (FORCE or CLOSE COMBAT), break free (FORCE), draw weapon, move zone

**Movement:** Move within zone = free; move to adjacent zone = fast action.

**Attacking:** Roll skill vs. 1 success to hit. Damage = weapon damage value in Conditions.  
Extra successes: +1 damage, outmaneuver (swap cards), stress enemy (mental Conditions), push enemy to another zone, make enemy drop item.

**Dodge/Parry:** Declare before opponent rolls. Each success removes 1 enemy success. Extra successes can swap initiative cards.

**Grappling:** FORCE test; success = target restrained (can only break free with FORCE opposed roll as fast action).

**Fleeing:** AGILITY test; success = exit combat. Opponent may spend fast action to contest.

**Ambush:** STEALTH vs. VIGILANCE; success = automatic initiative #1 (+1 per extra success to first action).

## Fear

When encountering vaesen or horror: roll **Logic or Empathy** (player's choice) + **+1 die per PC in scene** (max +3).

- Need successes equal to the **Fear value**
- Fail = **Terrified**: take mental Conditions equal to Fear value (each success reduces by 1)
- While Terrified: flee, freeze, faint, or frenzy (player's choice)
- After first Terrified turn: re-roll Fear each turn (no action) until scene ends

| Fear Value | Example |
|------------|---------|
| 1 | Bloody corpse, vaesen |
| 2 | Mass grave, violent assault |
| 3 | Truly unbearable horror |

## Vaesen in Combat

Vaesen use **MIGHT, BODY CONTROL, MAGIC, MANIPULATION** (not attributes/skills). Cannot push rolls.

- Also have **Fear** value (one-time on first encounter; some have two values e.g. 3/1)
- **Protection** works like armor (roll dice, each success = –1 damage)
- Have their own **Condition track** (listed per-vaesen in the bestiary); when Broken = per description
- Get 1 slow + 1 fast action per round (some fast vaesen draw 2 initiative cards or act twice)
- **Cannot be killed by normal weapons** — must identify weakness and perform ritual

### Magical Powers (3 types)
- **Enchantment** — mind-affecting magic
- **Curse** — lingering harmful effects
- **Trollcraft** — direct magical attacks/manipulation

## Resources & Gear

**Resources** = living standard (1–8). Roll Resources dice at start of mystery; successes buy temporary gear.  
Temporary gear is **lost** at end of mystery. Personal gear persists.

**Cost levels** 1–5. PCs can pool successes. Capital (wealth 5+) can substitute successes.

| Resources | Living Standard | Bonus | Capital |
|-----------|----------------|-------|---------|
| 1–2 | Destitute/Poor | –1/0 | 0 |
| 3–4 | Struggling/Stable | 0/+1 | 1–2 |
| 5–6 | Middle/Well-off | +1/+2 | 3–5 |
| 7–8 | Wealthy/Filthy rich | +3/+5 | 8–12 |

## Armor & Cover

**Armor:** Roll Protection dice when hit; each success = –1 damage. Also penalizes AGILITY.
- Light (leather) = 2 Protection, –1 AGILITY
- Medium = 4 Protection, –2 AGILITY
- Heavy = 6 Protection, –3 AGILITY

**Cover:** Same Protection roll; doesn't protect vs. melee (exception: explosions).

## NPCs

No separate physical/mental Conditions — use **Toughness** (each damage = –1 to relevant tests).

| NPC Type | Physical Toughness | Mental Toughness |
|----------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Minor | 1 | 1 |
| Named | 2 | 2 |
| Unique | 3 | 3 |

NPCs **cannot push rolls**.

## Mystery Structure

1. **Invitation** — letter, rumor, or contact draws party to location
2. **Journey** — short; each PC gets a scene to gain an Advantage
3. **Investigation** — explore locations, talk to NPCs, gather clues
4. **Confrontation** — face the vaesen; perform ritual to expel/banish
5. **Aftermath** — return to HQ; recovery rolls; DP questions; XP questions

**Rituals** don't require skill tests if performed correctly — the key is finding the right ritual.

## Special Rules Summary

**Helping:** Other PCs can assist — +1 die each, max +3 total.

**Opposed rolls:** Both roll; most successes wins. Tie = neither wins (GM may use consequences/Conditions/requirements). PC vs. PC tie: both may push.

**Difficulty modifiers:** GM may require extra successes for extreme circumstances (rarely; doesn't apply to mere atmosphere).

**Fire:** Damage per round (1/2/3 depending on extent); FORCE auto-test to reduce; AGILITY to extinguish (cumulative successes).

**Poison:** Toxicity 3/6/9+; opposed roll: GM rolls toxicity dice vs. your FORCE. Fail = Conditions equal to GM's successes, decreasing by 1 per subsequent round.

**Falling:** GM rolls (meters fallen – 2) dice; each success = 1 physical Condition. No armor protection.

**Hunger/Thirst/Fatigue:** FORCE test; fail = Condition (physical or mental, GM decides).

**Explosions:** Roll Blast Power dice; successes = damage to all in zone. AGILITY to dodge to adjacent zone. Cover protects even from same zone.
