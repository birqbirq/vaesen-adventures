## Return to Uppsala

We take the train back to Uppsala. It's late and [[Algot Frisk|Algot]] is asleep, so we turn in for the night.

Morning. Algot greets us with breakfast and coffee — and two letters that arrived while we were away.

### The Letters

**Yesterday — [[Linnea Elfeklint|Linnea]]'s Letter:**
> *"Dear friends,*
>
> *I have received an urgent message from our Danish comrades, and I fear that dark forces are at work. If there is any truth to the references to Sandemann, the situation is extremely serious.*
>
> *Kindly visit me at the asylum as soon as possible, and prepare to leave the country.*
>
> *Yours sincerely, Linnea"*

**The day after we left — [[Josef Strasser|Josef Strasser's Letter]] (received first):**
> *"To whom it may concern,*
>
> *My name is Josef Strasser. I am the Warden of [[Brygidki Prison]] in the city of [[Lemberg]], in the western part of Galicia. Lately, some unusual events have occurred here — several prisoners fell ill, and some of them have died rather gruesome deaths since then. I fear this streak might continue, and the thought that I can't do anything to help people who are my responsibility haunts me.*
>
> *I believe in the safety of my prison, and those deaths looked out of the ordinary. I am no expert on the supernatural and, therefore, I am in great need of your assistance.*
>
> *[List of prohibited items — no weapons, nothing sharp, no tobacco, no alcohol, no money or valuables, no medicine or drugs, no lighters or matches.]*
>
> *If you are able and willing to intervene in this matter, your attention will be greatly appreciated. As lives are endangered, time is of the essence.*
>
> *Most respectfully, Josef Strasser"*

We decide to go to Carpathia (received the Strasser letter first — first in, first out).

## The Researchers from the University

As we discuss travel plans, there's a knock at the door. [[Algot Frisk|Algot]] answers. Two men from the University — they want access to our library.

- **[[Dr. Djerf]]** — a philosopher with a doctorate. Algot recognizes the name. Abraham calls it out: someone in the Djerf family lost someone at the castle. Dr. Djerf admits it — his wife went missing. He's trying to understand what happened. Genuinely seems to be doing research into the occult and vaesen. Abraham reads him: intelligent in his field but surprisingly naive outside of it. Probably in on the book theft attempt, but was also genuinely interested in the conversation.
- **Alfred** — Dr. Djerf's teacher's assistant.

Abraham offers to give them a tour of the library and show them around. During the tour, **Alfred tries to pocket a book** — a first edition folklore text in immaculate condition, valuable but not occult. Abraham catches him (two successes on Vigilance/Logic) but lets it play out. As they're leaving, Abraham uses his walking stick to point it out diplomatically. Alfred returns the book. Abraham offers to let Alfred read it there under supervision — Alfred declines and they leave quickly. The two men fast-walk away from the castle.

Abraham asks Algot to quietly verify with the university whether Dr. Djerf actually teaches there.

### Prohibited Items at Brygidki Prison
Per Strasser's letter, these cannot be brought into the prison:
- Explosives or weapons of any kind
- Anything sharp (knives, scissors)
- Tobacco or tobacco products
- Alcohol or alcohol-containing food/drinks
- Money or valuables
- Medicine or drugs
- Lighters or matches

*The sword-cane is just a walking stick — technically allowed.*
