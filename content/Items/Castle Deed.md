# Castle Deed

## Overview
The deed to [[Castle Gyllencreutz]], signed by [[Linnea Elfeklint]]. Was nailed to the cabinet door in the castle's occult temple by [[Manfred]].

## Details
- [[Manfred]] had nailed the deed to the cabinet door in the occult temple — his power over the castle was tied to it
- [[Niklas Natt|Niklas]] took the deed from the door, which immediately broke Manfred's power: the ghost lights vanished and Manfred's spirit fled
- Linnea subsequently gave the castle to the party to rebuild [[The Society]]

## Adventure Appearances
- [[Adventures/1. Intro Session|Intro Session]] — taking the deed was the key action that defeated Manfred and resolved the adventure
