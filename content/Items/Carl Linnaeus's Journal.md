# Carl Linnaeus's Journal

## Overview
A handwritten journal by Carl Linnaeus found in the library of [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]. Contains the history of [[The Society]].

## Details
- Documents that [[The Society]] originated from the [[Order of Artemis]]
- Names Mats Rosenberg as one of the first members with the Sight
- Contains lineages of Society members — used by Abraham to trace [[Olaus Klint]]'s ancestry (a Rosenberg married a Klint)
- Also references the Hulu fire and its impact on the Society

## Adventure Appearances
- [[2 - Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — researched by Abraham to learn about Olaus Klint's background
