# Temple Key

## Overview
A rusty, ornate key found on the body of Ulf in the Laboratory on the 3rd floor of [[Castle Gyllencreutz]].

## Details
- Found clutched in Ulf's hand alongside a book (*Witchcraft and Immoral Practices in Southern Holland*)
- Has a temple-like symbol — described as fancy, elegant, important, hand-crafted; looks like a key to a church or temple
- Ulf had been dead no more than a few days when found; was screaming when he died

- [[Adventures/1. Intro Session|Intro Session]] — found on Ulf's body in the castle laboratory
