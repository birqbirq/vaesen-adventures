# Pocket Watch (Lieutenant)

## Overview
A pocket watch found in [[Coroner Volk|Coroner Esther Volk]]'s basement laboratory. Currently held at [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]. Potentially significant for a future adventure.

## Physical Description
- A pocket watch; age and condition not fully described
- **Engraved on the case** with a lieutenant's name — the first letter is **F**, the name contains an **E**, and the rest is worn and illegible
- **Inside the case:** a small photograph of a younger [[Coroner Volk|Esther Volk]] and a young man, both in military fatigues, both looking directly at the camera

## Known Details
- Found by [[Killian]] during his search of Volk's basement lab after the combat (Oct 16, 1897)
- The engraved name does not belong to Volk — it belonged to the **lieutenant in the photograph**
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] did not recognize the lieutenant
- Volk was from **Finland** and served as a **field medic** in the war — the photograph and the military fatigues are consistent with this background
- The watch may represent someone Volk was close to — possibly the person responsible for or connected to how she became a werewolf
- [[Killian]] noted the connection to [[National Archives]] records of werewolf rumors during the **Russo-Finnish War** period (~20 years prior to 1897)

## Open Questions
- Who is the lieutenant? What does the full name say?
- What was the relationship between Volk and this person?
- Is the lieutenant still alive?
- Is there a connection between the lieutenant and the origin of Volk's werewolf curse?
