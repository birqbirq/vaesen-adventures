# Friedrich Balm — Connection to Serial Murders

tags: #clue #soak #murders #npc #resolved

**Source:** Church scene investigation, Oct 14; séance; [[Gabriel Utterson]]; [[Father Rudolph]]

[[Friedrich Balm]] was the organist at a Stockholm church, residing in a room provided by Father Rudolph. He was murdered on **October 14, 1897** — the sixth victim of the serial beast killings. He is also now believed to be responsible for the murders of multiple young women found in the river near [[Whitehill]].

### How he died
- Struck at the top of the church stairs while heading up to play the organ for evening choir practice.
- Carried through a large stained glass window by the force of the blow (window shattered outward).
- Fell back down the stairs; found on the landing by church staff and police.
- Wound: abdomen torn open, intestines partially missing. More brutal than prior victims.
- Gray animal fur found on broken window frame — confirmed non-human.

### The notebook
Found beside the body. Contents:
- Charcoal portraits of young women — **no words, only drawings**.
- At least one subject identified as a woman on [[Coroner Volk]]'s slab (river victim).
- Pages had been torn out; embossed impressions revealed **street addresses in Whitehill** (stalking locations).
- Friedrich had **signed several drawings** with his name.

### His room
- Sparse and austere — no personal effects, only music books and Royal Academy of Music texts.
- A history of the organ contained **handwritten notes in Russian** (read by [[Niklas Natt]]) describing detailed observations of women — teachers, students, passersby. Long-standing predatory behavior predating Stockholm.
- A marginal **drawing in the same style** as the notebook confirmed it was his work.
- Handwriting in the organ book matched that of the notebook.

### Lock of hair
Found in his breast pocket — tied with a silk ribbon. Likely a keepsake from one of his victims. Bagged by [[Gabriel Utterson]] and sent to [[Coroner Volk]].

### Séance findings
Niklas contacted Friedrich's spirit. He:
- Didn't know who killed him or that he was dead until told.
- Remembered only walking up the stairs in the dark — nothing further.
- Delivered a message of gratitude for Father Rudolph.
- Was accompanied by two other spirits: a long-resident male (who greeted Friedrich to "purgatory") and an unidentified Polish/Eastern European woman who pointed toward the broken window.

### Significance to the case
- His murder fits the vaesen's MO: **targeting wrongdoers**. The vaesen appears to act as a vigilante predator, killing criminals.
- His death resolves the mystery of the missing maids (see [[Whitehill Limb Transplants]]).
- [[Sverker von Essen]] remains the only victim without a known criminal history — still the outlier.
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] noted the conductor ([[Mortimer Cronstedt]]) may need to be reconsidered as a connected case if Sverker had a motive.
