# Steamboat Schedules — Potential Lead

tags: #clue #soak #lead #steamboats

> [!warning] Status
> Likely moot — werewolf confirmed as the killer; steamboat schedule as a lead has been superseded.

**Source:** City Gazette, October 14, 1897

The murders follow a monthly pattern tied to the new moon (May–September). Steamboats running regular routes between Stockholm and other cities could place the same crew or passengers in Stockholm on each murder date. This is an uninvestigated lead.

## Schedules from the Gazette

### Steamer MARY (Gothenburg ↔ London)
Captain Thisleigh. Departs Gothenburg every other Tuesday; departs London every other Thursday.

| From Gothenburg | From London |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Oct 3, **Oct 17**, Oct 31 | Oct 12, Oct 26 |
| Nov 14, Nov 28 | Nov 9, Nov 23 |

- **Oct 17** departure from Gothenburg means the Mary arrives in Stockholm area around the time of the next predicted murder (~Oct 17–18).
- Past Gothenburg departures would have been: ~Sep 19, Sep 5, Aug 22, Aug 8, Jul 25, Jul 11, **Jun 27**, **Jun 13**, **May 30**, **May 16** — cross-referencing these against murder dates (May 23, Jun 22, Jul 21, Aug 19, Sep 18) could be revealing if the ship stops in Stockholm en route.

### Steamboat DANA
Departs Stockholm southbound **October 14** (today) at 8 a.m. from the Isle. No further schedule listed.

### Steamships FRANZÉN & NORE (Stockholm ↔ Wisby ↔ Kalmar)
Captain O. (Franzén), Captain F.W. (Nore). Regular trips on this route. Franzén departs on a Thursday in November — no specific past dates listed.

### Steamships Lt. TORSTENSSON & BRAVIKEN (Stockholm ↔ Lübeck)
Regular service stopping at Köping, Kalmar, and Ystad. No specific dates listed in the Gazette.

### Freighter HERMES
In service between Karlstad, Kristinehamn, and Gothenburg at first open water. Contact S.-P. Wiglus.

## Why this matters
- The murder dates (new moon, ~monthly) could align with a regular shipping schedule.
- A crew member or frequent passenger arriving in Stockholm each month would have opportunity for all five murders.
- The Mary's schedule is the most detailed — worth checking if it stops in Stockholm and whether its past arrival dates overlap with murder dates.
- The Dana leaving today means its crew was in Stockholm during the September 18 murder window.

## Open questions
- Does the Mary stop in Stockholm between Gothenburg and London?
- Can the party obtain passenger or crew manifests for any of these vessels?
- Were any of the four prior victims connected to the docks or waterfront areas?
