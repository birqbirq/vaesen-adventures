# Sverker von Essen — Death

tags: #clue #soak #murders #sverker

**Source:** [[Gabriel Utterson]], [[Coroner Volk]], séance with [[Sverker]]

[[Sverker|Sverker von Essen]] was found dead behind the [[Royal Opera]] on **September 18, 1897** — a new moon.

### Circumstances
- He was walking home after a performance of Verdi's *A Masked Ball* near [[St. James Church]].
- He had stepped outside to practice a note slightly outside his range — he liked to challenge himself.
- He sensed something watching him from the shadows before he was killed.
- His entire throat was destroyed in one powerful strike — voice box included.
- The attacker grabbed and missed once first (scratch marks on skin), then landed a single decisive killing blow.
- Died from blood loss.

### From the séance
- He did not know what was watching him.
- He had no enemies — was loved by all, accepted everyone.
- No gambling debts, no opium, no underworld ties.
- The only rumored tension: talk of replacing him with understudy [[Rufus Holberg]] as lead.
- He was singing *La Navarraise* (French opera) when killed.
- His wife is **Bernhardina**. He named himself as the person he loved most.

### Rufus Holberg
- Had a confirmed alibi — was at [[Bern's Salon]] with colleagues at the time of the murder.
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] found no real connection or bad blood between them beyond the rumor.

### Open questions
- What was watching Sverker from the shadows?
- Why did the killer target him specifically, breaking the pattern of criminal victims?
- Is the note he was singing significant — could a specific pitch have triggered or attracted the vaesen?
