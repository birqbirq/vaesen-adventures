# Serial Murders — Wound Pattern

tags: #clue #soak #murders #vaesen

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]], [[Gabriel Utterson]]

All five murder victims died from blood loss caused by violent tearing wounds. The injuries are consistent across all cases:

- Wounds are **external** — tearing from the outside in, not internal rupture. Scratch marks on the skin surface confirm this.
- The force required is far beyond what a normal human could produce.
- Tearing pattern resembles a **large canine or wild animal**, not a blade or blunt instrument.
- No weapon marks. No gunshot wounds.

### Wound locations by victim
| Date | Location | Wound |
|------|----------|-------|
| May 23 | Mire Lake | Kidney |

| Jul 21 | Shipside Street | Throat |
| Aug 19 | Ship's Lock | Throat |
| Sep 18 | [[Royal Opera]] | Throat (voice box destroyed) |

[[Sverker|Sverker von Essen]]'s wound was notably **cleaner and more decisive** than the others — one powerful strike. The earlier victims showed more signs of struggle and resistance.

### Implications
- The attacker grabbed and missed at least once before the killing blow on Sverker (scratch marks present).
- The escalating precision may suggest the killer is becoming more practiced, or that Sverker was a specific, deliberate target.
- The prior four victims were criminals from the [[Whitehill]] slums — treated by police as underworld violence. Sverker broke that pattern entirely.
