# Serial Murders — Wound Pattern

tags: #clue #soak #murders #vaesen

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]], [[Gabriel Utterson]], church scene investigation (Oct 14)

All six murder victims died from blood loss caused by violent tearing wounds. The injuries are consistent across all cases:

- Wounds are **external** — tearing from the outside in, not internal rupture. Scratch marks on the skin surface confirm this.
- The force required is far beyond what a normal human could produce.
- Tearing pattern resembles a **large canine or wild animal**, not a blade or blunt instrument.
- No weapon marks. No gunshot wounds.

### Physical evidence — Oct 14 scene
- A **tuft of gray fur**, matted with blood, was found on a sharp edge of the broken church window.
- The fur is **not human** — texture and clumping resembles the thick undercoat of a large dog (like a husky).
- Bagged and sent to [[Coroner Volk]] for analysis.
- The window was shattered **from the inside out** — all glass fell outside, frame bent outward. The killer exited through the window.
- No non-human footprints found on the floor — only human prints.
- Reconstructed sequence: killer emerged from darkness, struck [[Friedrich Balm]] at the top of the stairs, carried him forward through the window with the force of the blow, then leapt out. Friedrich's body fell back down the stairs.

### Escalation
- [[Sverker]]'s wound was notably **cleaner and more decisive** than the earlier victims.
- [[Friedrich Balm]]'s wounds were **more brutal** than any prior victim — the commissioner noted the killer may be becoming more frantic or losing control.
- This is consistent with werewolf lore: bloodlust intensifies around the full moon.

### Werewolf hypothesis
Based on the fur evidence and wound pattern, [[Abraham]] assessed what is known about werewolves in the Vaesen tradition:
- They **must** transform on the full moon; can also transform in days before or after if bloodlust is sufficient.
- Particularly vulnerable to **silver** objects and weapons.
- Enchanted wolf skin destruction can **kill or cure** the werewolf permanently.
- Calling the werewolf by their **Christian name once** may break the transformation and force them back to human form.
- The full moon was October 11th — the October 14th killing was 3 days later, within the transformation window.
