# Whitehill — Limb Transplants

tags: #clue #soak #whitehill #transplants

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]], [[Mutilation]] (the beggar)

The "disease" killing residents of the [[Whitehill]] slums is not a disease. Every corpse received from Whitehill has had a limb **amputated and replaced with a mismatched limb from another person**. The body rejects the foreign limb, causing fatal infection.

### What we know
- Someone is performing these transplants with **partial surgical skill** — not a fully trained professional, but not unskilled either.
- Victims are **paid** for the procedure (though poorly — the beggar received little).
- The limbs used are mismatched — not selected by size or gender.
- [[Coroner Volk]] suspects the limbs are sourced from bodies that "wouldn't be missed."
- Only a morgue or hospital would have the equipment. She does not believe [[Seraphim Hospital]] is responsible.
- Victims rarely reach the hospital in time — [[Whitehill]] is on the south side, the hospital is on the north side.

### The beggar ([[Mutilation]])
- His right hand is a transplant — ulcerated, pale, putrid-smelling, clearly not his own.
- He was approached **outside his hut** by a tall, well-dressed, clean-shaven man who seemed out of place in Whitehill.
- Was taken by carriage (lost track of the route), given an injection, and woke up in a park in Whitehill.
- Most others he knew who went through the procedure have since died.
- He would recognize the man if he saw him again.
- The party took him to [[Seraphim Hospital]] on [[August T. Lysander|Lysander]]'s tab.

### Missing maids connection
[[Coroner Volk]] has also received **5–6 bodies of young women** — all strangled, all found on the shoreline or in the river near [[Whitehill]]. No missing limbs. No names. She believes they are the missing maids reported by the Southside Police Department (see [[Maids Missing in the White Hills]]).
