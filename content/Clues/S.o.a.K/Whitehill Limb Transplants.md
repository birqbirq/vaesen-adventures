# Whitehill — Limb Transplants

tags: #clue #soak #whitehill #transplants #resolved

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]], [[Mutilation]] (the beggar), [[Dr. Tyko Gregorius]], physical evidence (Oct 15, 1897)

The "disease" killing residents of the [[Whitehill]] slums is not a disease. Every corpse received from Whitehill has had a limb **amputated and replaced with a mismatched limb from another person**. The body rejects the foreign limb, causing fatal infection.

### ✅ RESOLVED — Perpetrator Confirmed
**[[Dr. Jens Svardh]]** — senior physician at [[Seraphim Hospital]] — is confirmed as the surgeon behind the Whitehill transplants. His **leather satchel**, found at the fountain where he was attacked on Oct 15, contained surgical instruments and a notebook titled *"Transplantation of Extremities from Cadavers — Experimental Series 3A."* The notebook documents the experiments in detail.

Svardh has been captured (now in werewolf form) and is in police custody at [[Stockholm Police Headquarters]]. See [[Dr. Svardh]].

### What we know
- The transplants were systematic, deliberate **human experimentation** — documented in Svardh's notebook as "Experimental Series 3A"
- Victims were **paid** for the procedure (desperation drove acceptance)
- Limbs were mismatched — not selected by size or gender; sourced from bodies "that wouldn't be missed" (cadavers)
- The limbs had been dead ~4 hours before reattachment — they were never going to take
- Supplies required: opium, lye soap, possibly aspirin or cocaine (pain management)
- [[Coroner Volk]] does not believe [[Seraphim Hospital]] as an institution was responsible — Svardh appears to have been acting alone

### The beggar ([[Mutilation]])
- His right hand was a transplant — ulcerated, gangrene, putrid. Died at [[Seraphim Hospital]] during treatment on October 14th.
- Confirmed victims are paid; most others he knew died; he would have recognized the surgeon by sight.
- **Died before he could identify the perpetrator** — but the notebook makes identification moot.

### Missing maids — resolved separately
[[Coroner Volk]] had received 5–6 bodies of young women — strangled, found in the river near Whitehill. These were the work of **[[Friedrich Balm]]**, the church organist murdered Oct 14th. See [[Friedrich Balm - Connection to Serial Murders]].
