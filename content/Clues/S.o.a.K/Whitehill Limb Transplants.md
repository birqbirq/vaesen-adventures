# Whitehill — Limb Transplants

tags: #clue #soak #whitehill #transplants

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]], [[Mutilation]] (the beggar), [[Dr. Tyko Gregorius]]

The "disease" killing residents of the [[Whitehill]] slums is not a disease. Every corpse received from Whitehill has had a limb **amputated and replaced with a mismatched limb from another person**. The body rejects the foreign limb, causing fatal infection.

### What we know
- Someone is performing these transplants with **partial surgical skill** — technically precise, but not fully trained. Former army medic, autodidact, or someone with access to medical resources but no formal credentials.
- Victims are **paid** for the procedure.
- The limbs are mismatched — not selected by size or gender; sourced from bodies "that wouldn't be missed."
- The limbs used had been dead ~4 hours before reattachment — they were **never going to take**.
- Only a morgue or hospital would have the equipment. [[Coroner Volk]] does not believe [[Seraphim Hospital]] is responsible.
- [[Dr. Tyko Gregorius]] (junior doctor at Seraphim) has been treating victims — calls it "the pauper's disease." Most arrive too late to save.
- Supplies required: opium, lye soap, possibly aspirin or cocaine (pain management); these are hospital/pharmacy-controlled. An inside contact could divert them — but no known theft has been reported.
- Site indicators to look for: smell of death/decay, large trash bags, thick black smoke (incineration of amputated material).

### The perpetrator
- Described by [[Mutilation]] as: tall, well-dressed, clean-shaven, clearly out of place in Whitehill — referred to in the slums as "the fancy guy."
- No name known; no one refused him (desperation drove everyone to accept payment).
- **[[Dr. Svardh]]** — senior physician at [[Seraphim Hospital]], has been making visits to Whitehill — is a person of interest. Has not yet been interviewed.
- Hospital administrator **Anna** (last name unknown) may have records of supply irregularities — returns Monday.

### The beggar ([[Mutilation]])
- His right hand was a transplant — ulcerated, gangrene, putrid. Died at [[Seraphim Hospital]] during treatment on October 14th.
- Confirmed: victims are paid; most others he knew died; he would have recognized the surgeon by sight.
- **Died before he could identify the perpetrator.**

### Missing maids — now partially resolved
[[Coroner Volk]] had received 5–6 bodies of young women — strangled, found in the river near Whitehill. These are now believed to be the work of **[[Friedrich Balm]]**, the church organist murdered on Oct 14th. His notebook contained charcoal portraits of these women, and his room contained older surveillance notes in Russian describing similar victims. Whitehill street addresses were recovered from embossed impressions in the notebook — these were his stalking locations. See [[Friedrich Balm - Connection to Serial Murders]].

### Open questions
- Who is "the fancy guy"? Is it [[Dr. Svardh]]?
- Where is the surgery being performed in Whitehill?
- What is the purpose — experimentation? Something occult?
- Are there still living victims in Whitehill who could identify the perpetrator?
