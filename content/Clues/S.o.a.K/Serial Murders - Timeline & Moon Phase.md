### Moon phase reassessment
- The first five murders appeared to follow the **new moon** (29–30 day gaps).
- The October 14th murder occurred on a **full moon +3 days**, not a new moon.
- Per [[Abraham]]'s knowledge of werewolf lore: the beast turns **on the full moon without fail**, but can also turn in the days preceding or following if bloodlust is strong enough.
- The escalating brutality (Friedrich's injuries were more gruesome than prior victims; Svardh's wife described glowing green eyes and an overwhelming growl) is consistent with increasing bloodlust or loss of control.
- Possible re-interpretation: the earlier murders may also have been near full moons rather than new moons — dates need re-checking against a full moon calendar.

### ✅ Werewolf confirmed
The vaesen is a **werewolf**. Confirmed on Oct 15 when [[Dr. Svardh]] transformed at [[Seraphim Hospital]] after being attacked. **At least two werewolves** are active — a second howl was heard during the Oct 15–16 chase. See [[Werewolf (Stockholm)]].
