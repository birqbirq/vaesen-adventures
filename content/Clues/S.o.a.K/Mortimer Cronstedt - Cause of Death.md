# Mortimer Cronstedt — Cause of Death

tags: #clue #soak #murder #poison

**Source:** [[Coroner Volk]]

[[Mortimer Cronstedt]], conductor of the [[Royal Opera]], died approximately **late August 1897** (around August 28th) — roughly six weeks before the current in-game date.

### Official story vs. reality
- **Official:** Fatal botulism from turtle soup at the [[Golden Peace]] tavern. The Commissioner is publicly maintaining this explanation.
- **Actual:** Deliberately poisoned with an **opium-based substance at an extremely high dose**. [[Coroner Volk]] is certain it was murder.

### Why the cover-up?
[[Gabriel Utterson]] is suppressing the murder finding to avoid public panic, given the ongoing political unrest and protests against the government. A second high-profile murder at the opera would inflame tensions further.

### Open questions
- Who poisoned him, and how was the opium administered?
- Is his death connected to the serial killings, or a separate crime?
- [[Gabriel Utterson]] noted no known links between Mortimer and [[Sverker]] — but both were central figures at the [[Royal Opera]].
- [[Niklas Natt]] flagged: did anyone take out a life insurance policy on Mortimer?
