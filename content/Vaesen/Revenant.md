# Revenant

## Overview
A type of Vaesen — a ghost bound to the mortal world by unresolved injustice or unfinished business.

## Known Instances
- [[Oskar Hjort]] — haunted the [[Witch Cat Inn]]; caused nightmares in guests; possessed [[Sami Harjula|Sami]]; guided [[Sophia]] to tell his story via shadow theater

## Resolution (Oskar's case)
- Find the physical remains
- Bury them in consecrated ground — must be carried over the cemetery wall, not through the gate

## Adventure Appearances
- [[2 - Dance of Dreams|Dance of Dreams]] — Oskar Hjort identified as a Revenant after the mystery was resolved
