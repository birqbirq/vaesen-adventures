# Selkie

#vaesen #hamburg #carpathia

**Also known as:** Selchie, Silkie (Scottish), Wereseal

## Description

Selkies are seal-folk — shapeshifters who wear their seal skin when on dry land and become seals in the water. The transformation is tied to water, not the moon. Most commonly female, though males exist. Outside of water they appear as ordinary humans, distinguished only by their seal-skin garments (often worn as a cloak or cape).

They originate from Scottish and Orkney/Shetland folklore. The name "selkie" is derived from a Scottish word for seal.

## Nature

Non-hostile in nature. Curious, well-traveled, and observant — the ones encountered in Hamburg had spent years learning about the region and its people. Seem to possess a natural empathy and intuition about character.
