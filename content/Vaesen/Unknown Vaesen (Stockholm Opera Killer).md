# Unknown Vaesen (Stockholm Opera Killer)

## Overview
An unidentified Vaesen responsible for the murder of tenor [[Sverker]] at the [[Royal Opera]] in Stockholm. Currently being investigated in Adventure 3.

## Known Details
- Watched [[Sverker]] from the shadows while he practiced outside [[St. James Church]]
- Ripped open Sverker's throat when he hit a high note
- [[August T. Lysander]] believes the killer is not human

## Status
Unknown. Active. The party is traveling to Stockholm to investigate.
