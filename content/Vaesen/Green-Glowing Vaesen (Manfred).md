# Green-Glowing Vaesen (Manfred's Creature)

## Overview
An unidentified Vaesen associated with [[Manfred]] at [[Castle Gyllencreutz]]. Manifested as green ghost lights and a bat-winged creature. Type not formally identified.

## Details
- Appeared as green glowing lights; could charm and entrance people with the Sight
- Manfred himself transformed into a bat-winged creature with claws, commanding the ghost lights
- Used a large fermentation vat in the castle's occult temple to trap the souls of people with the Sight
- At least 3 souls were trapped (corresponding to 3 ghost lights / 3 missing people)
- A younger female ghost light was also present — charmed Dixon and Niklas on the 2nd floor stairs
- [[Linnea Elfeklint|Linnea]]'s description: *"think of fire, green, happy children with faces twisted in pain, suffering, glowing, permanently trapped"*
- Not a known type — the party had never heard of a green-glowing vaesen in a town (usually found in swamps or forests)

## Resolution
- Manfred's power was broken when [[Niklas Natt|Niklas]] took the castle deed from the cabinet door in the occult temple
- Manfred's spirit fled; the trapped souls were freed
