# Werewolf (Stockholm Serial Killer)

## Overview
The vaesen responsible for a series of murders across Stockholm. Confirmed to be a **werewolf** — this was established when [[Dr. Svardh]] transformed at [[Seraphim Hospital]] after being attacked. At least **two werewolves** are active in Stockholm (two distinct howls heard on Oct 15–16).

The werewolf appears to target those who prey on others — criminals, murderers, and wrongdoers. The one outlier remains **[[Sverker]] von Essen**, who had no known criminal record.

### Physical evidence
- Wounds are **external, tearing** — outside-in, far beyond human strength
- Gray fur (thick undercoat, large canine) found on broken church window (Oct 14 scene)
- **Green, glowing, angry eyes** — described by [[Dr. Svardh]]'s wife at the fountain; consistent with supernatural werewolf form
- Kills by ripping and tearing, not biting — though were-Svardh did bite [[Niklas Natt|Niklas]] during the Oct 16 fight

### Behavior
- Hunts at night; not seen in daylight
- Prefers to stalk prey and be **seen** — wants the victim to fear them
- Targets those who have committed crimes or harmed others (pattern)
- Escalating brutality — wounds are becoming more severe over time (increasing bloodlust or loss of control)
- The second howl on Oct 15 suggests two werewolves may be coordinating — or at minimum both are active

## Werewolf Lore (known to the party)
- Transforms **on the full moon** without fail; can also transform in days before/after if bloodlust is strong
- Most cannot control themselves in wolf form
- The bite can **spread the curse** — [[Dr. Svardh]] appears to have transformed after being attacked, not after the next full moon
- **Vulnerabilities:**
  - Silver weapons (confirmed effective — silver knife did 2 damage; silver bullet broke were-Svardh)
  - A close relative calling it by its **right name** (full human name) may break transformation
  - A third method exists that the party does not yet know
- **Lead bullets** are ineffective — cause pain and distraction but no lasting damage

## Dr. Svardh (Captured Werewolf)
- Attacked at the fountain on Oct 15; survived; transformed at the hospital
- Captured Oct 16 with silver weapons; in police custody at [[Stockholm Police Headquarters]]
- Whether he was already a werewolf before the attack is unknown
- May have been bitten/created by the **original werewolf** — the one responsible for the earlier murders

## The Original Werewolf (Unidentified)
- Responsible for the six murders prior to Oct 15
- Identity unknown
- The **second howl** on Oct 15 came from a different direction than Svardh — indicating a separate individual
- [[Sverker]] von Essen's death remains unexplained — why target someone with no criminal record?
- [[Professor Liebholz]] may know something he is not sharing
