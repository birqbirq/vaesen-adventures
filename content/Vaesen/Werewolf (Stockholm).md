# Werewolf (Stockholm Serial Killer)

## Overview
The vaesen responsible for a series of murders across Stockholm. **Both werewolves are now dead (Oct 16, 1897). The S.o.a.K. mystery is resolved.**

The original killer was **[[Coroner Volk|Coroner Esther Volk]]** — Stockholm's own city coroner, a Finnish field medic who became cursed during the Russo-Finnish War period. She targeted those who preyed on others: criminals, murderers, and wrongdoers. The one outlier remains **[[Sverker]] von Essen** — no clear criminal record was ever established, though the pattern of all other victims suggests Volk believed he had done something worthy of punishment.

[[Dr. Svardh]] was a secondary werewolf — bitten by Volk during the fountain attack on Oct 15, he transformed rapidly and killed two additional pedestrians before being captured and later killed.

### Physical evidence
- Wounds are **external, tearing** — outside-in, far beyond human strength
- Gray fur (thick undercoat, large canine) found on broken church window (Oct 14 scene)
- **Green, glowing, angry eyes** — described by [[Dr. Svardh]]'s wife at the fountain; consistent with supernatural werewolf form

### Behavior
- Hunted at night; not seen in daylight
- Targeted those who had committed crimes or harmed others (pattern — [[Sverker]] remains the outlier)
- Escalating brutality over time — wounds became more severe (consistent with curse-induced bloodlust)
- Volk had been chaining herself in her basement lab for some time and using a chemical suppression treatment; the treatment recently stopped working

## The Original Werewolf — Coroner Esther Volk ✅ Deceased
- **Confirmed as the serial killer** — responsible for murders May–Oct 1897
- Finnish field medic; came to Stockholm after the war
- Curse likely contracted during or after the **Russo-Finnish War** (~20 years prior)
- Connection to an unknown **lieutenant** (F---e--- engraved on a pocket watch) from her military service — may be how she became cursed
- Shot in the leg with a silver bullet by [[Killian]] on Oct 16, 1897; expired from the wound and exhaustion
- Utterson covering the death as natural causes
- **See also:** [[Pocket Watch (Lieutenant)]]

## Dr. Svardh (Secondary Werewolf) ✅ Deceased
- Bitten by Volk during the fountain attack on Oct 15; transformed rapidly — did not wait for the next full moon
- Captured Oct 16; escaped custody when iron shackles broke; killed by [[Dixon Hill]] in Volk's basement, shot through the heart with a silver bullet
- Reverted to human form in death

## Werewolf Lore (known to the party)
*Confirmed through research at the [[National Archives]] and direct experience*

- Transforms **on the full moon** without fail; can also transform in days before/after if bloodlust is strong
- **Voluntary werewolves** are far more bloodthirsty than those accidentally turned
- **Transformation triggers:** moon-dependent; can also be triggered by strong emotion (anger, fear, sadness, joy — even **happiness**)
- The bite can **spread the curse** — Svardh confirmed this, transforming rapidly after attack
- Some can **choose when to transform**; most cannot control it once started
- Some can **choose to become a bear** instead of a wolf
- Some can choose to be **invisible to humans** in wolf form; many prefer to be visible — the fear of the prey is part of the hunt
- Bloodlust **increases over time** — the longer a werewolf has been one, the more dangerous
- The **curse typically lasts 7 years**
- **Ways to end the curse:**
  1. **Silver weapons** ✅ Confirmed effective
  2. A **close relative calls it by its right name** (full human name) — may break transformation (Volk had no close relatives)
  3. The werewolf **attacks a pregnant woman, tears out the fetus, and consumes it** — recorded folk cure; party did not pursue
- **Lead bullets** are ineffective — cause pain and distraction but no lasting damage
- Historical records of werewolves/berserkers from Viking times; also rumors from the **Russo-Finnish wars ~20 years ago** — consistent with Volk's origin
