# The Society

## Overview
A secret organization of people with the Sight, dedicated to understanding and coexisting with Vaesen. The party has inherited its Uppsala headquarters and is rebuilding it.

## History
- Originally founded as the [[Order of Artemis]]; one of the first members with the Sight was Mats Rosenberg
- History documented in a handwritten journal by Carl Linnaeus, found in [[Castle Gyllencreutz|the castle library]]
- Suffered greatly in the Hulu fire in Uppsala, which destroyed records and killed most members
- After the fire, the Rosenberg lineage split off to form what became the [[Rosenberg Faction]], believing all Vaesen must be destroyed
- Dwindled over time due to danger, divisions, and defectors; eventually [[Linnea Elfeklint]] was the last surviving member
- The castle was left to Linnea and then given to the party to rebuild

## Beliefs
- Vaesen can be either good or evil — they are not inherently one or the other
- People with the Sight have a responsibility to understand and engage with Vaesen
