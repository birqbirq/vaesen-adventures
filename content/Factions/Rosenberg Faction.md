# Rosenberg Faction

## Overview
A splinter group that broke away from [[The Society]] after the Hulu fire. Believes all Vaesen are evil and must be purged.

## History
- Founded after the Hulu fire by the Rosenberg lineage, who concluded that all Vaesen are evil and of the devil
- Created a new, unnamed society led by a Finnish priest's daughter named Johanna Lampinen
- Per [[Pyri Harjula|Pyri]]'s séance: both societies (the Rosenbergs and the original Society) would end up in the same place and violence would ensue
- Later in Pyri's life, the Rosenberg society started dispersing
- [[Olaus Klint]] is a current representative — a direct descendant of the founding Rosenberg/Klint families

## Beliefs
- All Vaesen are evil and must be destroyed
- Opposed to [[The Society]]'s view that Vaesen can be good or neutral

## Known Members
- [[Olaus Klint]] — private detective; tried to recruit the party
