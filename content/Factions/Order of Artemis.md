# Order of Artemis

## Overview
The predecessor organization to [[The Society]]. Founded by Mats Rosenberg and others with the Sight.

## History
- One of the first members with the Sight was Mats Rosenberg
- Eventually renamed to [[The Society]]
- [[Olaus Klint]] is a direct descendant of the Order's founding families (a Rosenberg married a Klint)
- [[Pyri Harjula]] joined after the rename — was not a member of the Order of Artemis itself
